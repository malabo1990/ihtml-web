# IHTML Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 20 November 2026_

This policy explains what data **IHTML** (the "Controller") processes when you
visit the website, buy a subscription or use the IHTML and IHTML Mirror apps.
Contact for any privacy matter:
**[malabo1990@gmail.com](mailto:malabo1990@gmail.com)**.

**In short:** the website uses no cookies and does not store your IP; the app
never sends the content of your projects; payments are processed by Stripe and
we never see your card.

## 1. Website (ihtml.eu)

**First-party, cookieless analytics.** To know how many people visit the
website and download IHTML we record:

- the page visited, the language and the site you came from (domain only, for
  example `google.com`);
- the **approximate country and city**, derived from the connection by our
  provider (Cloudflare); **your IP address is not stored**;
- the device type and operating system (for example "mobile, Android");
- **how long the page is visible** and whether you click a download button;
- a **random tab identifier** (`sessionStorage`), deleted when you close the tab
  and not able to tell who you are.

We use no cookies, no browser fingerprinting and no third-party analytics or
advertising services. If your browser has **Do Not Track** enabled, nothing is
recorded. Legal basis: legitimate interest in understanding aggregate website
usage. Retention: **400 days**, then deleted automatically.

**What the website saves in your browser.** Only your language and theme (light or dark) choices (`localStorage`, so the site opens the way you left it next time) and the tab identifier above (`sessionStorage`). They are not cookies, they are never sent to us, and you can delete them from your browser settings. A small notice on the website explains this; closing it is also remembered in `localStorage`.

**Hosting.** The website is hosted on **GitHub Pages** and the analytics on
**Cloudflare**. Like any web server, they may process your IP technically and
transiently to deliver the page and protect it from abuse, under their own
privacy policies.

## 2. Buying the subscription (Stripe)

Payments are handled by **Stripe Payments Europe, Ltd.** When you subscribe,
Stripe collects your email, name, payment details and the country or address
needed to calculate taxes. **We never receive or store your card details.** We
receive from Stripe your name, email, country, subscription status and amounts.

Legal basis: performance of the contract and legal obligations (accounting and
tax). Retention: for as long as the subscription lasts and then for the period
required by law for accounting and tax records (generally **6 years**).

## 3. IHTML Pro licenses

To activate and renew Pro, the app sends our license server (on Cloudflare) the
**license key** and a **random device identifier**, generated on your device and
containing no personal data. Activated devices (up to 2) are stored in your
Stripe subscription. The server returns a signed permit that includes your name
(or email) to display it in the app. Legal basis: performance of the contract.
Retention: for as long as the subscription lasts.

## 4. The apps (IHTML and IHTML Mirror)

- Your projects are stored **on your device**. We never receive them.
- **Version 1.0 sends no telemetry.** If a future version adds diagnostic
  telemetry, it will be anonymous, will never include your project content, IP,
  name or email, and can be turned off in **Settings › Diagnostics**. This policy
  will be updated beforehand.
- The apps only connect to the internet to check the Pro license and for what
  you include in your projects (for example, a YouTube video).

## 5. Forum and contact

The forum is hosted on **GitHub Discussions** and governed by GitHub's privacy
policy: what you post there is public. If you email us, we will use your data
only to reply.

## 6. Who we share data with

Only with the providers needed to run the service, acting as processors or
controllers according to their role: **Stripe** (payments), **Cloudflare**
(license server and analytics) and **GitHub** (website, downloads and forum).
Some are in the United States; transfers rely on the EU-US Data Privacy
Framework or standard contractual clauses. **We do not sell your data** or use it
for advertising.

## 7. Your rights

You can request access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and
portability of your data by writing to
[malabo1990@gmail.com](mailto:malabo1990@gmail.com). If you think we have not
handled your request properly, you can complain to the **Spanish Data Protection
Agency** ([aepd.es](https://www.aepd.es)) or your local supervisory authority.

## 8. Changes

If we change this policy, we will publish the new version here with its date.
Material changes will be announced on the website or in the app.

_The Spanish version of this policy prevails in case of discrepancy._
